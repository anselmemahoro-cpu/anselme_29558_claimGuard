## Phase I – Business Process Modeling

**Selected business process:** Motor accident claim registration, assessment and settlement. A policyholder reports an accident, the Claims Officer records the incident and registers the claim, and the ClaimGuard system checks that the policy is active. If it is, the Assessor assesses the damage and the Claims Manager approves or rejects the claim. Approved claims are paid and closed by the Finance Officer. The process ends as rejected (inactive policy), rejected (manager's decision) or paid. Every change is written to an audit log.
